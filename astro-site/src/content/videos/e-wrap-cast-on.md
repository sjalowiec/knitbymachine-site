In this video, I'll show you how to do an E-wrap cast on for your knitting machine.

The E-wrap is one of the most common ways to start your knitting. It creates an open edge at the bottom of your work, which means you can unravel from this end later if you need to.

**Step by step:**

First, bring out your needles to the working position. I'm using about 20 needles for this demonstration.

Thread your yarn through the tension mast and hold the end in your left hand. Starting from the left side, wrap the yarn around each needle in a counter-clockwise direction when viewed from above.

The key is to keep your tension consistent but not too tight. If you wrap too tightly, your first row will be difficult to knit.

Once all needles are wrapped, hang your cast-on comb and weights. This helps hold the work down as you begin knitting.

Set your tension dial and slowly push the carriage across. Your first row is now complete.

**Tips:**

- If stitches are dropping, try wrapping a bit more snugly
- A light-colored yarn helps you see what's happening
- Practice on 20 needles before trying a full-width cast on
