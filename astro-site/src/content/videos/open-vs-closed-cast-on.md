What edge did you just make? This video explains the difference between open and closed cast-ons—and why it matters for your projects.

**Open cast-ons:**

An open cast-on (like the E-wrap) creates a loopy edge at the bottom of your knitting. The key characteristic: you can unravel from this end.

Open cast-ons are ideal when you:
- Need to pick up stitches later
- Want to graft two pieces together
- Might need to adjust the length

**Closed cast-ons:**

A closed cast-on creates a finished, sealed edge. You cannot unravel from a closed cast-on—the bottom is locked.

Use closed cast-ons when you:
- Want a neat, permanent edge
- Are making a finished hem or cuff
- Don't need any flexibility at the start

**How to tell the difference:**

Look at your cast-on edge closely. With an open cast-on, you'll see loose loops that could potentially be pulled out. With a closed cast-on, the edge is firmly interlocked.

**Choosing the right one:**

For beginners, start with the E-wrap (open) because it's forgiving and easy to redo if needed. As you gain experience, you'll learn when a closed cast-on serves your project better.

The most important thing is knowing what you've made so you can plan your finishing accordingly!
