Let:

rowsInput = query rows (number)

unit = query unit ("in" | "cm")

Convert to per-inch internally:

If unit = "in" (inputs are over 4 inches)

rowsPerIn = rowsInput / 4

If unit = "cm" (inputs are over 10 cm)

Convert to per-inch:

stsPerIn = stsPerCm * 2.54

rowsPerIn = rowsPerCm * 2.54

Then compute true-scale cell size in inches:

cellW = 1 / stsPerIn

cellH = 1 / rowsPerIn
