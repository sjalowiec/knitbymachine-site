------------------------------------------------------------
WEDGE CROWN MATH (SEGMENTED)
------------------------------------------------------------
For wedge crowns:
- totalStitches = cast-on stitches at crown start
- wedgeCount = 4 or 6
- wedgeStitches = totalStitches / wedgeCount
  If not divisible, distribute remainder by adding +1 stitch to the first few wedges; reflect that in text as “work wedges as evenly as possible”.

- Each decrease row reduces the wedge by 2 stitches (1 at each edge).
- Choose targetStitchesPerWedge = 2 (default for now; classic finish)
- requiredDecreaseRows = floor((wedgeStitches - targetStitchesPerWedge) / 2)

Crown instruction should use requiredDecreaseRows.

Note: If wedgeStitches is small, ensure requiredDecreaseRows does not go negative.

WEDGE CROWN WORDING (MACHINE-NATIVE)

Replace any “groups across the row” / “Decrease Row repeat for each group” language for wedge crowns.

For 4-wedge option, output:

Crown Shaping
4-Wedge Crown (Knit Wedges Separately)

Decrease 1 stitch (definition): Transfer the next stitch to its neighboring needle, then shift stitches to fill the empty needle.

Work {bodyRowsAfterBrim} rows in pattern after the brim.

Work one wedge at a time:
- Scrap off / place on waste yarn 3/4 of the stitches, leaving 1 wedge ({wedgeStitches} stitches) on the machine.
- Shape the wedge by decreasing at BOTH edges every other row:
  Decrease Row: Decrease 1 stitch at the left edge AND decrease 1 stitch at the right edge. (2 stitches decreased total)
  Knit the row.
- Repeat the decrease row every other row {requiredDecreaseRows} times, until {targetStitchesPerWedge} stitches remain for that wedge.
- Scrap off the finished wedge stitches.

Rehang the next wedge stitches and repeat until all 4 wedges are complete.
Finish: Thread yarn through the remaining crown stitches and pull tight to close the top; secure.

For 6-wedge option, same structure but:
