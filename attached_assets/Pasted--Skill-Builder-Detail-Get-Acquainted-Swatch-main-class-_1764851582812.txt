        <li>Set your tension dial somewhere in the middle of the range as a starting point.</li>

    <div class="kbm-step-panels">
      <!-- Step 1 -->
      <article id="step-1" class="kbm-step-card kbm-step-card--active">
        <header class="kbm-step-card__header">
          <span class="kbm-step-card__step-number">Step 1</span>
          <h3 class="kbm-step-card__title">Getting Oriented</h3>
        </header>
        <p class="kbm-step-card__why">
          Take a moment to notice where everything is before yarn ever touches a needle.
        </p>
        <ul class="kbm-step-card__list">
          <li>Slide the carriage gently from left to right and back to feel the resistance.</li>
          <li>Look at the needle bed and find the position marks (A, B, C, D if your machine has them).</li>
          <li>Check that your yarn mast and tension unit are assembled and upright.</li>
          <li>Place your yarn cone or ball where it can feed smoothly without snagging.</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

      <!-- Step 2 -->
      <article id="step-2" class="kbm-step-card">
        <header class="kbm-step-card__header">
          <span class="kbm-step-card__step-number">Step 2</span>
          <h3 class="kbm-step-card__title">Cast On & First Rows</h3>
        </header>
        <p class="kbm-step-card__why">
          Get stitches on the machine with a simple, forgiving cast-on.
        </p>
        <ul class="kbm-step-card__list">
          <li>Thread your yarn through the mast and carriage following your machine manual.</li>
          <li>Bring out 30–40 needles to working position in the center of the bed.</li>
          <li>Use your preferred simple cast-on (e-wrap, weaving, or a comb cast-on).</li>
          <li>Knit 6–8 rows at your starting tension, watching for clean, even stitches.</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

      <!-- Step 3 -->
      <article id="step-3" class="kbm-step-card">
        <header class="kbm-step-card__header">
          <span class="kbm-step-card__step-number">Step 3</span>
          <h3 class="kbm-step-card__title">Play with Tension</h3>
        </header>
        <p class="kbm-step-card__why">
          See how the same yarn behaves at different settings so you can choose confidently later.
        </p>
        <ul class="kbm-step-card__list">
          <li>Knit 10 rows at your current tension and note how the fabric looks and feels.</li>
          <li>Change the tension dial one full number tighter and knit another 10 rows.</li>
          <li>Change the tension dial one full number looser than your original and knit 10 rows.</li>
          <li>Compare the three sections: which looks most balanced for this yarn and machine?</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

      <!-- Step 4 -->
      <article id="step-4" class="kbm-step-card">
        <header class="kbm-step-card__header">
          <span class="kbm-step-card__step-number">Step 4</span>
          <h3 class="kbm-step-card__title">Add a Simple Pattern</h3>
        </header>
        <p class="kbm-step-card__why">
          Try one very simple texture so you can see how your machine handles stitch changes.
        </p>
        <ul class="kbm-step-card__list">
          <li>Choose a basic pattern your machine can do easily: tuck, slip, or every-other-needle.</li>
          <li>Set up the needles or patterning as directed in your manual.</li>
          <li>Knit 10–20 rows in the pattern, watching for any problem areas.</li>
          <li>Return to plain knitting for a few rows to stabilize before finishing.</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

      <!-- Step 5 -->
      <article id="step-5" class="kbm-step-card">
        <header class="kbm-step-card__header">
          <span class="kbm-step-card__step-number">Step 5</span>
          <h3 class="kbm-step-card__title">Finish & Label Your Swatch</h3>
        </header>
        <p class="kbm-step-card__why">
          Capture what you learned so this swatch becomes a reference, not just a practice piece.
        </p>
        <ul class="kbm-step-card__list">
          <li>Knit a few final plain rows, then bind off loosely.</li>
          <li>Steam or block the swatch lightly if your yarn allows.</li>
          <li>On a tag or small note, record yarn, machine, and the tension settings you tried.</li>
          <li>Attach the note to the swatch so future-you can use this as a guide.</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

<script>
  (function () {
    const chips = document.querySelectorAll('.kbm-step-chip');
    const cards = document.querySelectorAll('.kbm-step-card');

    chips.forEach(chip => {
      chip.addEventListener('click', () => {
        const target = chip.getAttribute('data-step-target');

        chips.forEach(c => c.classList.remove('kbm-step-chip--active'));
        chip.classList.add('kbm-step-chip--active');

        cards.forEach(card => {
          card.classList.toggle('kbm-step-card--active', card.id === target.substring(1));
        });
      });
    });

    const celebrateBtn = document.getElementById('kbm-celebrate-btn');
